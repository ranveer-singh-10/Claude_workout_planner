## How to write workouts
- Format: Warm-up (time) → Strength/skill → Conditioning (EMOM/AMRAP/etc.) → Mobility/cool-down, with a time for each block.
- Give sets, reps, rest, suggested loads (kg or % of benchmarks) or RPE, and a scaled option for each movement.
- Include a [20–30] minute "minimum viable" version for disrupted days.
- Build plans in 4-week blocks with progressive overload and a lighter week every 4–6 weeks.
- Balance the week across push, pull, legs/hinge, core and conditioning.
- Suggest 5–10 minute mobility routines for non-training days.
